## Overview

Harborline is a complete admin dashboard for boat charter companies, marinas, and boat clubs, built with React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, and shadcn/ui.

## Features

- **Interactive slip map** – three piers, 50 slips, click any slip for its boat and status
- **Departures timeline** – today's trips with captains, guests, and booking status
- **Fleet tracking** – utilization, engine hours, and service-due progress bars
- **Revenue analytics** – charters vs. slip rentals, compared against last year
- **Maintenance work orders** and **crew roles** (Owner, Dockmaster, Captain, Deckhand)
- Light and dark mode, fully responsive
